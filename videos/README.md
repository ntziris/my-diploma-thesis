## What each recording shows

1. **Connect a Web3 wallet.** Connect Slush and choose an application role.
2. **Update supervisory parameters.** Change the royalty setting through the administration interface.
3. **Issue an artwork.** Upload artwork files, encrypt the high-resolution image and issue the artwork.
4. **Record gallery custody.** Confirm receipt of the physical artwork at the gallery.
5. **Issue a certificate of authenticity.** Attach gallery documentation and issue the certificate.
6. **Create fractional ownership.** Split ownership of a certified artwork into fractions.
7. **List fractions for sale.** Create a marketplace listing with a quantity and price.
8. **Buy fractions and pay the artist royalty.** Complete a purchase and inspect the payment breakdown.
9. **View a fraction in a Web3 wallet.** Inspect the fraction object through the wallet interface.
10. **Access the high-resolution image with Seal.** Authorize access and decrypt the artwork image in the browser.
11. **Register an external source.** Create a source in the simulator and register it through the administration interface.
12. **Submit an external condition report.** Send source readings and inspect the resulting on-chain report.
13. **Declare an artwork lost or destroyed.** Record the gallery declaration and inspect the final artwork state.
14. **Burn a fraction of a lost artwork.** Remove a fraction after the artwork has been declared lost.
